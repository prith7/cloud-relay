How it works

Live in three simple steps

The workflow is designed for creators, channels, and teams who want a reliable always-on stream without a dedicated machine.

1

Choose your destination

Select YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, or enter a custom RTMP server and stream key from your platform dashboard.

2

Upload your video

Drop in a pre-recorded MP4 or supported video file. Turn on looping if you want it to run continuously.

3

Go live from the cloud

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. Once your video is uploaded and the stream starts, the broadcast runs from the cloud. You can close your browser or shut down your device.

Which platforms can I stream to?

You can stream to platforms that accept RTMP input, including YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, and custom RTMP destinations. Availability depends on the platform's own rules and your account status.

Do I need OBS or streaming software?

No. CloudRelay is designed for pre-recorded video streaming through a browser workflow. You upload a file, provide your stream key, and start the stream.

Can I loop a video for 24/7 streaming?

Yes. Looping is intended for always-on formats like music radio, ambient channels, podcasts, and information boards.

Is this a copy of Streamza?

No. This is an original demo website inspired by the structure and product category of cloud-based pre-recorded live streaming services. It uses different branding, copy, and implementation.
